Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

SentryLoot is operated by Asliddinov Shukhratjon. For privacy questions or requests, contact compliance@gamestorehub.com.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Facebook, Meta provides the information you approve, including your name, profile picture, primary email address, and Facebook account identifier. SentryLoot does not receive your Facebook password. We use this information to create and secure your account, sign you in, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent accounts.

When you use the marketplace, we also process the account profile information you provide, listings and messages you submit, and records of requests, orders, disputes, and account security events.

How information is used and shared

We use account information to provide sign-in, marketplace features, customer support, fraud prevention, and dispute handling. Meta processes Facebook Login and returns the fields you approve. Service providers that host and operate SentryLoot may process data on our behalf to run the site and database. We do not request access to your Facebook friends, posts, or other optional profile information.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while the account is active. After a verified account deletion request, a daily automated process removes direct profile identifiers, Facebook sign-in links, identity verification data, and user-authored marketplace content within 30 days. Completed order and ledger records are retained in anonymized form where needed for accounting, security, or dispute records. See our data deletion instructions.

Cookies and security

SentryLoot uses a session cookie to keep you signed in and protect account actions. Passwords are stored as password hashes. Facebook access tokens are used server-side to retrieve the approved profile and are not stored as account credentials.

Your choices

You can update profile details in your account, request account deletion from the account page, or revoke SentryLoot's access in your Facebook settings. Deleting your SentryLoot account does not delete your Facebook account.

This notice describes the current service. Contact compliance@gamestorehub.com with privacy questions.
