Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

SentryLoot is operated by Asliddinov Shukhratjon. For privacy questions or requests, contact sentryloot@gamestorehub.com.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Facebook, Meta provides the information you approve, including your name, profile picture, primary email address, and Facebook account identifier. SentryLoot does not receive your Facebook password. We use this information to create and secure your account, sign you in, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent accounts.

When you use the marketplace, we also process the account profile information you provide, listings and messages you submit, and records of requests, orders, disputes, and account security events.

For game-account purchases, a buyer may be asked to submit two screenshots showing access to the delivered account. Screenshots are re-encoded to remove embedded image metadata and stored in a private storage bucket. Only authorized moderators receive short-lived access links to review them. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, payment data, email/phone details, identity documents, or another person's information. These screenshots show access at one time; they do not establish exclusive ownership or prevent later account recovery.

How information is used and shared

We use account information to provide sign-in, marketplace features, customer support, fraud prevention, and dispute handling. Meta processes Facebook Login and returns the fields you approve. Service providers that host and operate SentryLoot may process data on our behalf to run the site and database. The Crisp chat widget loads on the site; if you use it, Crisp processes your chat messages and related technical data for support. See Crisp’s privacy policy. Internal automated text checks flag signs of off-platform contact or credential requests for human review. They do not make decisions about accounts, listings, disputes, or payments. We do not request access to your Facebook friends, posts, or other optional profile information.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while the account is active. Account-purchase screenshots are deleted 30 days after a moderator resolves the review; a verified account deletion process also removes that buyer's stored screenshots. After a verified account deletion request, a daily automated process removes direct profile identifiers, Facebook sign-in links, identity verification data, and user-authored marketplace content within 30 days. Private purchase screenshots are deleted when a verified deletion is processed, or automatically 30 days after a moderator resolves the review. Open evidence reviews are retained until resolved plus 30 days. Completed order and ledger records may be retained in an anonymized form only as needed for accounting, security, or dispute handling. The exact statutory retention period depends on the countries served and has not yet been legally established; paid sales remain disabled pending that review. See our data deletion instructions.

Cookies and security

SentryLoot uses a session cookie to keep you signed in and protect account actions. Passwords are stored as password hashes. Facebook access tokens are used server-side to retrieve the approved profile and are not stored as account credentials.

Your choices

You can update profile details in your account, request account deletion from the account page, or revoke SentryLoot's access in your Facebook settings. Deleting your SentryLoot account does not delete your Facebook account.

This notice describes the current service. Contact sentryloot@gamestorehub.com with privacy questions.
