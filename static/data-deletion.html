Data Deletion Instructions

SentryLoot is operated by Asliddinov Shukhratjon. If you used Facebook Login, SentryLoot may hold your Facebook account identifier, name, profile picture, and primary email address.

Request deletion while signed in

  1. Sign in to SentryLoot and open your Account page.
  2. Choose Request account deletion.
  3. We verify the request. A daily automated process erases your direct account identifiers, Facebook Login link, identity verification data, and user-authored marketplace content within 30 days of the request.

When an administrator resolves a verified request earlier, the same erasure runs immediately. The account is disabled; its Facebook identity and direct profile data are erased; and user-authored content is removed or anonymized. Completed order and ledger records may remain only in anonymized form when needed for accounting, security, or dispute handling. The exact statutory retention schedule varies by country and has not yet been approved by counsel; live paid sales remain disabled until this review is complete.

Any private screenshots you submitted for a game-account purchase are also deleted from storage when the verified request is processed. Otherwise, those screenshots are automatically deleted 30 days after a moderator resolves the review.

If you cannot access your account

Email sentryloot@gamestorehub.com from the address associated with your account and ask for SentryLoot account and Facebook data deletion. Do not send your Facebook password. Email requests are identity-checked and handled within 30 days of verification.

Deleting SentryLoot data does not delete your Facebook account. You can also remove SentryLoot from the apps and websites connected to your Facebook account.
