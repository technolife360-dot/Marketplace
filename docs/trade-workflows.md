# Order fulfilment and support

Payment integrations and Top Up suppliers remain unchanged and disabled for live payments.

- Sellers set an IANA time zone, weekly opening hours and vacation mode in the seller workspace. Empty hours mean 24/7. The promised delivery time starts at the next opening and adds the listing's delivery duration. The schedule, delivery duration, recipient field definitions and after-sale policy are captured when the order is created. Later edits do not rewrite an existing order's promise. Vacation blocks new checkout but preserves existing orders.
- Manual item, currency and service deliveries collect a player/character identifier, with a server/zone for games that require one, plus optional platform and meeting details. These identifiers are encrypted and visible only to the buyer, the seller of that line and moderators. Buyers can correct them until the first delivery. Sellers cannot deliver a new line until required fields are filled.
- Each delivery is an immutable batch with a quantity, encrypted result/reference and retry key. Progress is cumulative, such as 6/10. Retrying the same key does not add quantity twice. Completion and account verification require every line to be fully delivered. Existing single deliveries migrate as complete lines.
- Any product type can attach text results and private JPEG/PNG/WebP evidence to its order. Photos use the existing private evidence bucket, normalized images and signed URLs. Passwords and OTPs do not belong in evidence. A storage failure is reported without claiming the photo was uploaded.
- Buyers can request cancellation separately from a dispute. Unpaid orders release reservations immediately. Paid orders require every seller's consent; disagreement, any partial delivery or an unanswered 48-hour request sends the matter to moderators. Pending requests pause delivery and completion. The buyer may withdraw a request. A moderator may reject cancellation to resume fulfilment or approve a sandbox refund. No real refund is invented when an adapter is absent.
- Ordinary disputes retain their dedicated case page, private evidence, participant comments, 48-hour response target and moderator decision. Cancellation and after-sale cases have a separate moderator queue.
- New orders record an after-sale reporting period: 14 days for accounts, 7 for other products, starting at completion. Covered issues are seller-caused account reclamation, goods not as described, missing items/currency, invalid codes and unperformed services as appropriate to the product. Buyer-caused problems and unrelated publisher actions are excluded. This is a support and investigation period, not an automatic refund or financial guarantee. Legacy orders are explicitly marked without retroactive coverage.
- A participant can appeal a final dispute, account-verification or after-sale decision within 7 days by supplying new evidence. Each participant gets one appeal per source decision. Replies, evidence and moderator actions are recorded. Revising a decision opens a follow-up state; a moderator closes it only after recording the completed next steps. These actions do not automatically move money.
- Users can save up to 30 filtered catalogue searches, toggle on-site alerts and remove saved searches. Publication matching reuses the catalogue's predicates and production visibility gates. Existing published listings do not trigger new-listing alerts, and each listing alerts each saved search only once.

## Operations

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261019230000_trade_workflows.sql` before serving the new code on PostgreSQL. Runtime migration is additive and repeatable. All new tables enable RLS and revoke browser-role privileges; the existing server session, CSRF, verification and role checks control access. Workflows use the request's database connection and row locks.

The existing `/api/cron/order-deadlines` job also escalates overdue cancellation requests. Opening an order performs the same overdue check, so a seller or buyer need not wait for the daily scheduler. New evidence is retained while an after-sale or appeal case is active; closed-order photos follow the existing 30-day retention job. Account deletion clears buyer identifiers and removes or anonymizes user-submitted workflow content.

The moderator queue is `/#order-cases`; saved searches are `/#saved-searches`.
