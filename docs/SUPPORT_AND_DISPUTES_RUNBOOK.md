# Support and disputes operating procedure

This is the internal procedure for the owner of `sentryloot@gamestorehub.com`. On 2026-10-18 the owner confirmed that they are the primary support and dispute operator. Coverage hours and a backup operator have not been assigned. Before accepting real orders, assign a backup and confirm daily inbox and Crisp chat coverage, including weekends. Do not publish response-time promises until those shifts are staffed.

## Intake and triage

1. Record a case ID, receipt time, account/order ID, contact channel, issue type, and operator. Keep passwords, OTPs, identity documents, payment-card data, and delivery secrets out of tickets and email.
2. Acknowledge account-access loss, suspected fraud, exposed credentials, or active account recovery as urgent. Internal target: first human review within four hours. Other order issues: within one business day. These are operating targets, not customer guarantees.
3. Ask the user to submit only the minimum evidence needed through the private order flow. Buyer account screenshots must be redacted and reviewed through the admin queue; never request them through public listing images or chat links. If evidence cannot be collected privately, pause the decision and explain the limitation.
4. Preserve the order, messages, listing snapshot, provider references, and audit IDs. Record a short factual chronology and the policy version that applied when the order was placed.

## Decision path

1. Check whether the buyer paid, the seller delivered, the buyer acknowledged receipt, and any review or dispute deadline. Distinguish an unfulfilled order, access failure, publisher ban, recovery by a previous owner, and buyer preference change.
2. Contact both parties through the platform. Give each a chance to explain and provide permitted evidence. Do not send account secrets or one party's private evidence to the other.
3. If a game publisher prohibits the underlying transfer, pause similar listings and escalate to the owner. A moderator must not infer ownership or publisher permission from Didit identity verification or a screenshot.
4. The moderator records a reasoned decision in the admin dispute queue, including evidence considered, action, amount if applicable, and conflict-of-interest check. A second operator reviews high-value or contested decisions before any real refund or payout instruction.
5. Confirm the actual provider refund/payout result separately. The current sandbox ledger and demo payment path do not prove a real money movement. Notify both parties of the final status and how to appeal.

## Escalation and incident record

- Security or privacy incident: restrict affected access immediately, preserve relevant audit IDs, rotate exposed credentials, and notify the owner. Record time, impact, containment, recovery, and follow-up in a private incident log.
- No primary response within the internal target: backup operator takes over. If no backup operator is assigned, keep real sales closed.
- Review open cases daily; review unresolved cases and aging weekly. Do not close a case merely because a platform status changed.
- Monthly drill: create a disposable sandbox order, open a dispute, submit redacted evidence, request more information, decide with a written reason, inspect the audit record, and verify that no real funds moved. Remove test data afterward.

## Launch ownership record

Before opening real sales, record privately: the owner's full name as primary operator, a backup operator, coverage hours and timezone, escalation contact, provider refund authority, and the date of a successful end-to-end drill. Only the owner's role is confirmed; their name and the remaining fields have not been supplied.
