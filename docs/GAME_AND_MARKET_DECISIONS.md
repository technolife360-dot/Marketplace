# Game and market launch decisions — review 2026-10-18

The production sale gate and per-game allow-lists remain closed. A country choice in the UI is not a legal or supplier approval. This register records only conclusions supported by an official source. Recheck sources and obtain operator/legal review immediately before opening any country-product pair.

Other games are not implicitly allowed. A new game's slug is blocked until its publisher rule, exact product type, review date, official source, and approving operator are recorded. Top-up availability must be reviewed separately from account-transfer rules and must match an authorized supplier product, permitted region, and required player identifier fields.

## Country and product gate

For each proposed buyer country, seller country, and product type, record the local operator eligibility, tax and consumer rules, KYC/privacy/retention obligations, supplier permission, payment/refund/payout coverage, dispute contact, and user-facing notice. Record a named reviewer and review date. Start with a small approved set; the current aim of “all Asia” is not an approval for every Asian jurisdiction. Countries with unresolved sanctions, platform access, or provider coverage must remain blocked.

The legal entity is not registered yet. Do not put a future company name or registration number into legal pages as an established fact. Obtain local professional review of the exact operator and launch countries before real sales.
